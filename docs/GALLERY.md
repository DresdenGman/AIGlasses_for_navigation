## Image provenance

The approved concept drawing was AI-generated during design exploration. Every image above was subsequently rendered from the actual v0.4 mesh, with consistent geometry across angles. The scenes and [render manifest](../design/v0.4/render_manifest.json) are included so that others can inspect or reproduce them. No identifiable community participants or fabricated endorsements appear in this gallery.
