# Outcome cases — individual moments from the participant survey

These seven cases are drawn from the project's published participant survey
([full results, method and limitations](FEEDBACK.md); raw data in
[`data/feedback/`](../data/feedback/)). They are individual moments, published
next to the aggregate numbers on purpose:

- **85 responses** (49 in Chinese, 36 in English); **64** respondents had used
  the glasses at least once.
- Mean helpfulness among those 64 users: **3.47 / 5**.
- **9 of the 64** were still using the glasses at survey time.
- The most-reported problem was **inaccurate prompts (48% of users)**.

How the cases were selected:

- **68 of the 85** respondents consented to anonymous quotation. Only those
  respondents are quoted here — nobody else, in this document or anywhere in
  this repository.
- Of the 68, 38 wrote a specific "most helpful moment"; 36 of those were
  people who had actually used the glasses. The seven cases below were chosen
  from those 36 for concrete, checkable detail.
- Not every response was filled in by the respondent. Across the survey, 51 of
  85 responses were entered by a family member or volunteer (34), recorded by
  an activity organiser (14), or completed another way (3). Each case below is
  labelled with how its response was completed; a proxy-entered quote is a
  record of what the user said, written down by someone else, and it is
  labelled that way rather than presented as first-person writing.
- Every quote is verbatim. Where an English rendering follows a Chinese quote,
  it is marked as a translation. No case combines details from different
  responses, and no names, ages or places beyond the session label are given —
  the survey did not collect them.
- These are moments from the top of the distribution, **not** the median
  experience. Read them with the aggregates above, not instead of them. Each
  case also carries the friction the same respondent reported, unedited.

## The cases

### 1. 降压药换了盒子（读药）
- 场次：老年活动中心场｜帮助度 4/5｜现状：使用比以前少｜家人或志愿者代填
- 场景选项：看标识、文字或周围环境信息；在家里测试
- 原话："闺女把降压药换了个盒子，我摸着拿不准，戴上眼镜它给念出来了，这下省得我天天戴老花镜凑在灯底下瞅。"
  （EN: "My daughter had moved my blood-pressure pills into a different box and I couldn't be sure by touch. I put on the glasses and it read it out for me — saved me from squinting under a lamp with reading glasses every day."）
- 同一份里的摩擦："镜腿那块儿发热，戴久了捂得慌。按键也是，摸半天不知道按着哪儿了。"
- 证据点：读药是刚需场景；起作用的是"念出来"，不是识别本身——省去的是每天重复的求助动作。

### 2. 药签和公交站牌（读字）
- Session: Community Center Session A｜Helpfulness 5/5｜Still using｜Self-completed
- 原话："Reading medicine labels and bus stop signs for me."
- 同一份里的摩擦："The battery drains in less than an hour of continuous use."
- 证据点：本人填写、仍在使用、满分——但同一人给出的续航数字（连续使用不到一小时）是全表最差之一。个案的说服力来自两句同行。

### 3. 楼道里的纸箱（避障，且被触摸验证）
- 场次：街道残联体验场（借机试用）｜帮助度 5/5｜现状：仍在使用｜家人或志愿者代填
- 场景选项：看标识；熟悉的室内空间移动；室内找物品；家里测试
- 原话："在楼道里走路能提前报前面堆着的纸箱，摸着确实在那，免得踢到绊脚。"
  （EN: "Walking in the corridor it warned me about the stacked cardboard boxes ahead in good time. I reached out and they really were there — saved me tripping over them."）
- 同一份里的摩擦："不经用，下楼买个菜的功夫就快没电了。"
- 证据点："摸着确实在那"是使用者自己做的验证——提示与现实对上，信任就是这么建立的；这也是感知评测里"宁可不报、不可乱报"口径的用户侧版本。

### 4. 台阶和咖啡杯（先于手杖）
- Session: Senior activity centre demo｜Helpfulness 5/5｜Still using｜Self-completed
- 原话："I took it home to try around my porch and living room. It was genuinely neat hearing it call out the steps and my coffee mug before my cane touched them."
- 同一份里的摩擦："The battery pack gets pretty warm in my pocket after half an hour, and my wife was a bit uneasy about having the camera on in the house."
- 证据点："before my cane touched them"一句说清了这个设备相对手杖的增量：提前量。摩擦同样具体——发热与家属对摄像头的顾虑，都是产品问题不是表态。

### 5. 没撑好的自行车（户外预警）
- 场次：社区活动中心体验场｜帮助度 3/5｜现状：使用比以前少｜家人或志愿者代填
- 场景选项：在熟悉的室内空间移动；户外短距离试用
- 原话："在小区楼下散步，前面停了辆没撑好的自行车，它提前两米滴滴报了，没撞上去。"
  （EN: "Walking downstairs in our compound, a bicycle that hadn't been propped up properly was ahead — it beeped a warning about two metres out, and I didn't walk into it."）
- 同一份里的摩擦："外面风一刮或者大卡车过去，声音就全给盖住了，耳机声音要是能调得更大点就好了。"
- 证据点：本集唯一的户外近失个案，且评分只有 3 分——有用的时刻和"用得比以前少"可以是同一个人。摩擦指向户外音频，这与问卷里 28% 报音频问题同向。

### 6. 冰箱里的牛奶和果汁（区分相似物）
- Session: Community centre session 2 (unit kept for testing)｜Helpfulness 4/5｜Still using｜Self-completed
- 原话："Helped me tell apart the milk carton from juice in the fridge."
- 同一份里的摩擦："Hard to hear the speaker when the kitchen fan is running."
- 证据点：不是"找到"，是"分清"——两个手感几乎一样的盒子。这类区分任务是触觉替代不了的，也是读字能力的日常形态。

### 7. 先听它说，再自己确认（独立性）
- 场次：老年活动中心场｜帮助度 5/5｜现状：仍在使用｜其他方式填写
- 场景选项：室内找物品；看标识、文字；家里测试；熟悉的室内空间移动；户外短距离试用
- 原话："最近在家里找东西，会先听它说，再自己确认。不是每次都急着问家人了，算是多一个办法。"
  （EN: "Lately, when I'm looking for something at home, I listen to what it says first and then check for myself. I don't rush to ask my family every time anymore — it's one more way of doing it."）
- 同一份里的摩擦："目前还顺手，没碰到什么明显毛病。"
- 证据点：本集的行为变化个案：从"问家人"变成"先听它说、再自己确认"。使用者给自己的定位很克制——"多一个办法"，不是替代。这句话适合做整组的收尾。

## Alternates（备选，同样已核同意）

- 找杯子和钥匙："Finding my cup and keys on the dining table quickly."（5/5，仍在使用，本人填写，Blind Association Workshop；摩擦：续航短、家人担心室内摄像头）
- 前面有人："Telling me when someone is standing in front of me."（4/5，仍在使用，家人代填；摩擦：断连要女儿帮忙重连）
- 菜市场招牌："早上去菜市场买菜，在摊位前面能报出来招牌上的大字。"（4/5，家人代填；摩擦：人多识别卡壳、没逛完就低电量）
- 绕长桌："在活动室试的时候报了前面的长桌子，自己试着绕过去没碰上。"（4/5，本人填写；摩擦：反应速度跟不上步速）

## Rules for using these cases

1. Anonymous form only. The case number and session label are the entire
   identification; never merge details from different responses into a
   composite "typical user".
2. Quotes must not be altered. A translation shown next to a quote must be
   marked as a translation.
3. The cases may only appear alongside the aggregate results (3.47/5
   helpfulness, 9 of 64 still using, 48% reporting inaccurate prompts). They
   are a slice from the top of the distribution, not the average.
4. The proxy-completion labels must not be dropped.
