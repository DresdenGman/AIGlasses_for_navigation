## 标题 Title
Low-cost AI navigation glasses — bilingual build guide for assistive makers

## 一句话描述 One-liner
A bilingual (Chinese/English) open maker guide for building a sub-US$30 assistive navigation prototype — with an honest bill of materials, a beginner assembly card, troubleshooting sheet, and a Low-Confidence Behavior Code that requires the device to say "I don't know" instead of inventing instructions.

## 详细介绍 Description
This build guide documents a low-cost, open-source AI navigation glasses prototype (hardware cost under US$30) designed to be reproducible by community makers. It includes:

- A bilingual bill of materials with honest cost scoping (measured vs. estimated figures clearly labeled; no supplier quotes invented);
- A step-by-step assembly card for beginners (60–90 minutes, with checkpoints and common mistakes);
- A one-page troubleshooting sheet;
- A Low-Confidence Behavior Code: five rules requiring the device to report uncertainty as non-actionable when confidence is below 0.70 — never issuing directional commands when unsure.

Honest context: this is a pilot community project, not an evaluated program. The author made contact with 4 residential communities in China (reaching nearly 60 visually impaired or low-vision residents through neighborhood committees) and ran hands-on build workshops in 3 of them; a structured follow-up is in progress. The device is a research prototype — not clinically validated, not certified, and not a replacement for a white cane, guide dog, or orientation-and-mobility training. It serves a specific population, not a general audience, and the guide does not overclaim safety or efficacy.
