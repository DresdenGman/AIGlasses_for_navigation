# Code of conduct

This is a small assistive-technology research project. People who build it, discuss it, or might be affected by it include blind and low-vision people, community partners, students, and maintainers.

## Expected

- Comment on the work. Disagreement about design and safety is fine; insults are not.
- Describe the prototype honestly. Do not call it a certified aid, a medical device, or a replacement for a cane, a guide dog, or orientation-and-mobility training.
- Leave other people's names, health details, recordings, locations, and community contacts out of issues, pull requests, and commits unless they have agreed to that publication.
- Keep existing attribution for upstream work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, slurs, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private messages, recordings, or contact details.
- Pressure to treat demo output as an instruction to move or cross a street.

## Maintainers

Maintainers may edit or close comments, issues, and pull requests that break these expectations, and may block repeated abuse. A question about a moderation decision can go in a GitHub issue that does not include private personal data. Vulnerability reports belong in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public argument.
